---

## 1. Motivation

> *(~200 words — fill in during Week 10)*

Standard defensive statistics — pass breakups, tackles, interceptions — fail to capture how well a defensive back actually *moved* to contest a throw. A defender who gives up a 30-yard gain but was in perfect position looks identical in the box score to one who was badly beaten. With player tracking data, we can do better.

This project introduces a framework to quantify individual defender skill during the window when the ball is in the air, isolating true ability from play context and scheme.
